EU-funded research · Horizon-EIC-2024-PATHFINDER

THREAD — Design-for-Demise Spacecraft

A multi-partner consortium developing technology to ensure spacecraft break apart safely and completely during atmospheric re-entry. My work focuses on Thermite-for-Demise (T4D) charges: formulating reactive materials and shaping them through casting and additive manufacturing.

  • Partners: Airbus Defence & Space, DLR, ENEA
  • My role: reactive materials formulation and charge shaping via additive manufacturing

M.Sc. Thesis — Shaping Thermites

Investigated casting and 3D printing routes for producing thermite charges for spacecraft demise, comparing manufacturing precision, repeatability, and burn characteristics between the two approaches.
