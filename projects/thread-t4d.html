THREAD — Design-for-Demise Spacecraft

EU-funded research (Horizon-EIC-2024-PATHFINDER) and M.Sc. thesis work on Thermite-for-Demise (T4D) technology for spacecraft re-entry.

THREAD consortium · Airbus Defence & Space, DLR, ENEA

Development of a Thermite-loaded Polymer Manufacturing Route for Spacecraft Design-for-Demise

Read the M.Sc. thesis

THREAD is a multi-partner European consortium developing technology to ensure spacecraft break apart safely and completely during atmospheric re-entry, reducing the risk of surviving debris reaching the ground. My work within the project focuses on Thermite-for-Demise (T4D): reactive material charges designed to trigger and accelerate the structural breakup of a spacecraft during re-entry.

Specifically, I developed a repeatable advanced manufacturing methodology for shaping thermite charges using two complementary routes: casting and Vat Photopolymerization (DLP 3D printing) of Mg/SiO2 energetic reactive composites. The goal was to produce charges with controlled geometry and predictable burn behavior, suitable for integration into a demisable spacecraft structure.

This work also forms the basis of my M.Sc. thesis, "Shaping Thermites: Investigation of Casting and 3D Printing for Spacecraft Demise Applications", completed at Politecnico di Milano, which compares the two manufacturing routes in terms of precision, repeatability, and burn characteristics.
