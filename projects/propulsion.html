Course Project, MSc · Space Propulsion · "The Fellowship Of The O-Ring"

Preliminary Design of a Bipropellant Semi-Cryogenic Blowdown Liquid Rocket Engine

A 1000 N LOX/RP-1 liquid rocket engine using a gas-pressurized blowdown architecture, in which propellant delivery pressure decays over the burn. The study focused on quantifying the resulting performance degradation, sizing a regenerative cooling jacket, and evaluating the impact of additive manufacturing surface roughness on discharge coefficients.

Continuous non-linear transient fluid-dynamic sizing (MATLAB) modeled how thrust and specific impulse degrade over the burn as tank pressure drops, benchmarked against nominal and experimental feature bounds.

The additively manufactured thrust chamber introduces geometric uncertainty at the injector level; cylindricity measurements on printed hole and pin components were compared against predicted and observed tolerances.

B.Sc. Thesis · Supervised by Prof. C. Paravan

A-10 Thunderbolt II Propulsion Analysis

A numerical modeling suite (MATLAB) simulating the baseline Joule-Brayton cycle of the TF34-GE-100 high-bypass turbofan engine that powers the A-10 Thunderbolt II, optimizing the compressor pressure ratio and validating velocity triangles against aerodynamic stall boundaries.

Increasing the compressor pressure ratio from the baseline βc = 14 to an optimized βc = 20.9 shifted the thermodynamic cycle to higher peak temperatures and pressures, improving cycle efficiency.

Course Project, MSc · Supervised by Prof. F. Maggi · Team of ~10, 6-month project

Preliminary Design of an Intercontinental Civil Vehicle for Rapid Payload Delivery

Conceptual scaling and trajectory optimization of a sub-orbital solid rocket motor launcher (Orion 50XL / STAR 37FM / STAR 30BP configuration) designed to deliver a 100 kg cargo payload across intercontinental range, using Modified Newtonian Flow Theory and ANSYS Fluent CFD to characterize supersonic and hypersonic aerodynamics through max-q.

Hypersonic aerodynamic characterization used a CFD mesh of the full vehicle to map surface force distributions, including local effects on the nose cone at Mach 20, alongside a center-of-pressure / center-of-gravity stability analysis across the flight envelope.

The resulting trajectory reaches an apogee near 1,900 km over a ~9,000 km downrange distance, with dynamic pressure and re-entry heat flux constraints shaping the first-stage burn and payload capsule design.
