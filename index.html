Aerospace Eng. — Propulsion, Energetic Materials, Additive Manufacturing & UAVs

I'm an M.Sc. Aeronautical Engineer from Politecnico di Milano, working in the fields of propulsion, unmanned systems, energetic materials, and 3D printing for aerospace and defense applications. My research experience is rooted in reactive material science for aerospace applications, with a strong focus on experimental testing and verification. I spent one year in the EU-funded THREAD consortium, where I developed my experimental master's thesis at the Space Propulsion Laboratory. As I begin my career, I'm looking to grow in R&D or hands-on testing roles, working directly with hardware and developing my experimental skills further across propulsion, materials, or unmanned systems.
